# Prompts — props e tiles das Catacumbas de Brumavale (dungeon dos esqueletos)

**Como usar:** anexe na IA as 2 imagens de referência (`ref-cripta-estilo.png` e `jogo-floresta.png`) e cole UM prompt por vez (cada um já traz o estilo completo). Me devolva os PNGs grandes: eu recorto, reduzo para a escala do jogo e ajusto a paleta.

## Folha 1 — mobiliário funerário (props grandes)

```
Create a pixel art sprite sheet for a top-down 3/4 view dark-fantasy RPG (similar to Pixel Crawler by Anokolisa / 16-bit). Match EXACTLY the art style of the attached reference images: crisp pixel art, NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO gradients, NO smooth shading, hard pixel edges; a 1-pixel dark outline in very dark blue-brown (not pure black); 3 to 4 shade steps per material, soft cel shading, light coming from the TOP-LEFT and shadow at the bottom-right; a limited, slightly desaturated palette: cold blue-gray stone, teal-blue brick, bone off-white, dim candle gold, rusty iron, dried blood red, with tiny accents of arcane purple and ghostly cyan; same camera angle and level of detail as the reference dungeon tiles, props and skeletons; every object isolated on a flat transparent (or plain #ff00ff magenta) background, evenly spaced in a grid, never touching or overlapping; no text, no labels, no baked ground shadows, no watermark; consistent scale between objects (the skeleton in the reference is about 64 px tall, a stone sarcophagus is about as long as a person). Theme: an ancient forgotten CRYPT / CATACOMBS under the village of Brumavale — cold, dark, haunted, dignified and a bit creepy, not gore.

Sheet 1 — funerary furniture (each object in its own grid cell, sizes at game scale):
1. 4 stone sarcophagi: (a) closed with a carved effigy lid, (b) closed ornate with a skull relief, (c) lid slightly ajar showing a dark gap, (d) cracked and broken with bones peeking out; about 56x34 px each
2. 5 gravestones: rounded cross, plain slab, broken top, angel relief, skull engraving; about 20x28 px each
3. 3 old wooden coffins: closed, lid ajar, and standing upright; about 28x44 px
4. 3 stone statues: a weeping angel with bowed head, an armored knight with a sword planted in front, a perched gargoyle; about 28x56 px
5. 1 stone sacrificial altar with dried blood stains and melted candles, about 56x36 px
6. 4 stone columns: 2 intact and carved, 2 broken at different heights; about 20x64 px and 20x40 px
7. 3 burial urns: clay jar, stone urn with lid, cracked jar; about 14x18 px
8. 1 small sealed mausoleum door with an arch and a skull above it, about 64x72 px
```
