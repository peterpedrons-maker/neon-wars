## Guerreiro

```
Create a pixel art icon sheet matching EXACTLY the style of the attached reference image (skill icons of a 16-bit fantasy RPG):
- crisp pixel art, NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO smooth gradients, hard pixel edges
- each icon is a square plate (draw each at 64x64 px, delivered 4x larger, so 256x256) with a very dark background (near-black navy with a faint radial glow behind the symbol) and a 2-pixel colored border
- one bold, simple central symbol filling about 70% of the plate, 4 to 5 flat colors with a bright highlight and a darker shadow side, light from the TOP-LEFT, small sparkle pixels for magic
- border color by theme: red/orange = damage and fury, blue = protection and defense, green = healing and nature, gold = wealth, experience and drops, violet = magic, time and shadow, cyan = speed and control
- CORNERSTONE icons (the 7th of each row) have a gold border, a more elaborate symbol and a small gold star in the corner
- icons in the same row share a color theme but each has a different symbol
- flat plain #ff00ff magenta background between icons, equal spacing, grid of 7 columns x 3 rows, no text, no labels, no numbers

Class: WARRIOR. Row 1 (red, FURY): 1 Heavy Strike (clenched fist with a spiked gauntlet), 2 Hot Blood (flaming drop of blood), 3 Impetus (axe with speed lines), 4 War Scars (crossed scar slashes), 5 Carnage (bloody skull with an axe), 6 Ferocity (roaring bear head), 7 Berserker CORNERSTONE (horned helmet with glowing red eyes).
Row 2 (blue, BASTION): 1 Thick Skin (muscular arm with a shield-like skin), 2 Reinforced Armor (steel breastplate), 3 Hard Shell (turtle shell), 4 Second Wind (wind swirl with a heart), 5 Thorns (spiked shield), 6 Iron Resistance (potion in an iron flask), 7 Bulwark CORNERSTONE (tower shield with a castle wall).
Row 3 (gold, COMMAND): 1 Impact (shockwave fist), 2 Brutal Fall (cracked ground under a falling figure), 3 War Loot (bag of coins on a spear), 4 Veteran (medal with a ribbon), 5 Treasure Hunter (treasure chest with a magnifier), 6 Counterattack (sword and shield with return arrows), 7 Tyrant CORNERSTONE (crown on a sword).
```
